Ukrainian spelling dictionary (uk_UA) for Hunspell
==================================================

Ukrainian spelling dictionary (uk_UA) from the dict_uk project (ВЕСУМ - Великий
електронний словник української мови; VESUM - Large Electronic Dictionary of
Ukrainian), https://github.com/brown-uk/dict_uk, release v6.8.6
(hunspell-uk_UA_6.8.6.zip, published 2026-09-19). uk_UA.aff and uk_UA.dic are
unmodified copies of the files in that release.
